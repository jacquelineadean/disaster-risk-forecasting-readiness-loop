County briefsread from briefs/<fips>/<period>.json

Every sentence cites something.

A county brief turns a validated probability into a paragraph a planner can read: the chance of at least one damaging event in the county this period, the score the model earned on the untouched test years, and what the county holds. Every sentence cites a card, an issued file, a pinned extract or a named guidance document, and the brief is written only when readiness.cite finds no violation.

Briefs are built by readiness brief --county FIPS --period 2026-Q4 (or --state XX) from issued/ files and the pinned USA Structures counts; none ship in the repository, because every one depends on a passing test card that only the real-data run can write. A brief names nothing below the county, never says what an event "would touch", and is never a warning product: official alerts come from the NWS and IPAWS. The rules are in docs/brief.md.
